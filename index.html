Why your business needs them

Customers decide in seconds who to trust and who to call. Each product below removes one reason they walk away.

NFC Google review cards

The problem: Most people check Google ratings before they visit or call. Happy customers rarely leave a review because it takes too many steps.

  • One tap opens your review page, so more happy customers actually post
  • More and better reviews make new customers trust you faster
  • A strong review profile helps you show up in local search

Best for: clinics, salons, restaurants, shops, contractors, any local business.

NFC business cards

The problem: Paper cards get lost or never saved. When a prospect can’t find your number later, the lead is gone.

  • Share your number, website, WhatsApp and socials with one tap
  • Looks premium and shows your business is modern and organized
  • No app needed on the other person’s phone

Best for: owners, sales teams, consultants, architects, anyone who meets new clients.

Catalogs

The problem: Customers can’t buy what they can’t see. Explaining every product and service again and again wastes your time.

  • Show your full range clearly in one professional document
  • Send it on WhatsApp or print it, so customers decide faster
  • Every salesperson presents the same consistent brand

Best for: traders, manufacturers, interior and construction firms, service providers.

Letterheads

The problem: Quotes and letters on plain paper look unofficial, and clients, banks and tender offices notice.

  • Every quote, invoice and letter carries your brand and contact details
  • Builds credibility with new clients and official bodies
  • A small cost that makes your business look established

Best for: any business that sends quotes, invoices or official letters.
